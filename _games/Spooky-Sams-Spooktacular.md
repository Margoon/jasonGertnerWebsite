## Description
Play as the Spooky Sam and kill everyone. 2 levels of fun!

## Thoughts
Made in a Halloween themed game jam in just 6 hours. In the game you play a ghost and can possess dogs and people to then kill other people. It's got some funny effects, but it's super short with just 2 levels. This was the first game jam I did in college!
