## Description
Kite Kyle may have been the first to be bashed in Cloud Bashers, but he will be the one to have the last laugh! Play as Kite Kyle and soar through the air and fight four bosses!

## Thoughts
The sequel/spin-off to Cloud Bashers! Originally it was made for a 48 hour game jam in college, a couple months after the release of Cloud Bashers. I decided why not do something based on the last game I made. It had new audio and some new art, but it reuses alot of assets from Cloud Bashers. It's a strange game with strange controls, was put on mobile for a bit before I decided to remove it. It was a little lower quality then the games I was making for SJG. It's tecnically a cancelled game, even though it was finsihed, it's just short.
