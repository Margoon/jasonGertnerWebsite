## Description
Play through the seasons to defeat an unknown power. Make allies? Defeat foes? Can you really make a difference? It's your adventure, why not try it?

## Thoughts
Made for the Resist Jam, a strange small game where you go through a few dungeons, solve some small puzzles, and fight against 2 bosses. Also going through seasons as you play. First you start with winter, and after defeating the ice wizard, he joins your party and you can switch between you're main guy and him. Ice guy shoots icicles and can freeze the floor, while main guy shoots green bullets. In the spring dungeon there's more enemies and some text on screen introducing some story. After that, you go to summer to fight a giant eyeball. The last room is fall, where all seems lost and it's just a room that kills you, but if you survive long enough, you get a special message of hope!

This was the first time I had someone do audio for my game. Jeff McMillen did a great job!
