## Description
You hot? You attract all the babes? You always are able to fix them up? Well this is the game where you DONT do that because of your jobs on the line! Fix all the pipes before its too late, avoid our lovely lady, and don’t get caught!

## Thoughts
This was made for Global Game Jam in 2020, and the theme was Repair. We had the idea that you play as a repair man in a wacky scenario where you have to not sleep with the client. It plays kind of like a horror game, where the client will slowly follow you around the room, and if she's close enough to you, she can seduce you. If you're fully seduced, you're fired and it's game over. But if you manage to fix all the leaky pipes with no funny business, you win!

This project had 2 programmers in addition to me, so I took more of a design focus, designing the layout and making some of the 3D props. I also recorded some saxophone that plays whenever you repair a pipe!
