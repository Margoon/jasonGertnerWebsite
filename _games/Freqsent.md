## Description
Play as a frequency wave and try and get your message sent!

## Thoughts
Made for Global Game Jam in 2018, where the theme was "Transmission". The game idea we went with was a platformer where you play as a message that's being sent. You could switch between different frequency wave types that give different abilities (high jump, dash, and going through static). It's pretty short, pretty simple, and there's a fun shader in the background. The game ends, potentially promising more levels in the future, but we never came back to it after the jam. This was also the first Global Game Jam I participated in!
